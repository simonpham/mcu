The Full Policy

This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Media Converter Pro: Ultimate ("the App"). We have designed this App with your privacy in mind. The core functionality of the App—converting your media files—is performed entirely on your device and does not require an internet connection. No personal data or media files are ever transmitted from your device to our servers.

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information about you. All media conversion processes are handled locally on your device. The App does not require access to your personal information such as name, email address, or phone number to function.

As a free, ad-supported application, the App may collect certain non-personally identifiable information, such as the type of device you are using, operating system version, and general usage statistics (e.g., app crashes). This information is used solely for the purpose of maintaining and improving the stability and performance of the App.

The App is supported by advertisements. To serve these ads, third-party ad networks may collect and use certain non-personally identifiable information. This information may be used to provide personalized ads to you. The data collected by these ad networks is governed by their own privacy policies.

We use the following ad partner. We do not have control over the data collected by them. Please review their privacy policy for more information:

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Because the App's core functionality is local and does not transmit your personal data or media files, the risk of external security breaches is minimized. While we strive to protect the data on your device, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

The App does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that the App has collected personally identifiable information about their child, please contact us immediately at the address provided below, and we will make every effort to promptly remove such information from our records.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
